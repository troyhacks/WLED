Display

The board can drive a MIPI-DSI panel on its connector, or an LT8912B bridge to a monitor. It works out which one is there by looking at the I2C bus, but it cannot tell one DSI panel from another - they all look the same from here - so the panel is named here.

HDMI mode:
The HDMI mode only matters when a bridge is attached: it is the size the monitor is driven at. The monitor's own EDID wins over it.
